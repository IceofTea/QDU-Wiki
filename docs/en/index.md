## About the University

**Qingdao University** is a key comprehensive university in Shandong Province and a "Double First-Class" construction university. Its history dates back to **1909** when the Qingdao Special Higher School was founded. In 1993, it was formed by merging four institutions: the former Qingdao University, Shandong Textile Engineering Institute, Qingdao Medical College, and Qingdao Normal College. The university now has **28 colleges and the Qingdao Medical School**, **90 undergraduate programs**, covering **11 discipline categories**, with over **41,000 enrolled students** and more than **500,000 notable alumni**.

## About This Site

This site is maintained by all members of [Baidu Tieba's QDU Bar](https://tieba.baidu.com/f?kw=%E9%9D%92%E5%B2%9B%E5%A4%A7%E5%AD%A6) and subsequent community supporters. It is an **unofficial campus survival guide**. It originally came from a Word document circulated in freshman groups, and through the word-of-mouth and continuous improvement of generation after generation of seniors, it gradually grew into what it is today — from New Student Guide to Campus Life, from Academics to Campus Services, covering every aspect of your life at QDU.

There are no cold official documents here, only heartfelt words from those who've been there. It doesn't make decisions for you, but only wishes to illuminate the bumps and stars on every path. If you find errors or have new experiences, you're welcome to contribute via the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/IceofTea/QDU-Wiki) to help this guide continue growing.
