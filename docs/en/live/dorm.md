## Campus Overview

Qingdao University covers an area of 2,496 mu with over 41,000 full-time students. In 2025, the university invested heavily to complete the **installation of 7,566 air conditioners**, achieving **full coverage** of air conditioning in student dormitories! This is a huge change; from now on, QDU dormitories say goodbye to the era without air conditioning. Meanwhile, the new student dormitory project on Fushan Campus (Buildings A, B, C, expected to provide about 4,847 beds) has started construction, with a construction period from June 2025 to 2027, when living conditions will further improve.

## General Information

- **Air Conditioning**: Since 2025, **all dormitories have air conditioners installed**, achieving full air conditioning coverage in student dormitories!
- **Heating**: All dormitories are equipped with heating; Qingdao winters are cold but indoor temperatures are warm.
- **Electricity**: Dormitories have **24-hour power supply**, voltage 220V. **High-power appliances are strictly prohibited** (hair dryers, curling irons, electric cooking pots, and other heating appliances cannot be used; they will cause power cuts, requiring processing at the college and dormitory management office to restore power).
- **Internet**: Campus network (QDU-1X and QDU-Web) requires internet service registration. Dormitories can have wired connections (consult customer service for details). Campus network is free for the first month, then requires payment. Account is student ID, password is last ten digits of ID card.
- **Bed Assignment**: Dormitories and beds are randomly assigned before student registration, with a notice posted on the door. For special needs (too tall, overweight, mobility difficulties, etc.), contact your counselor at registration for adjustment.
- **Curfew**: Dormitories have curfew; generally, **you must return before 11:30 PM**, otherwise you may not be able to enter. Entry and exit require swiping campus card.
- **Room Checks**: No teacher checks at night, but students (class committee members) will check dormitory headcount.
- **Dormitory Inspections**: Freshman management is relatively strict; maintain cleanliness, with daily duty for trash disposal and sweeping.
- **Bedding**: The mattress dimensions provided by the university (optional order) are 1.9*0.95 (m) for reference.

## Dormitory Dimensions and Configuration

- **Bed Dimensions**: 90cm × 190cm (can apply for extension if length is insufficient)
- **Cabinets**: Usually one per person, some dormitories have multiple per person. Space approximately 100cm×100cm×100cm, no dividers (can be purchased separately)
- **Ladder**: Square iron rod
- **Floor**: Varies by dormitory building, cement or tile; floor mats can be placed but are difficult to maintain
- **Sockets**: Available beside each bed, but limited to low-power uses like charging

## Dormitory FAQs (Q&A)

### Dormitory Management

**Q: What time do dormitory lights go out? Is there a curfew?**
A: Dormitories **do not have lights out**, you can stay up late. But there is **curfew**, generally **must return before 11:30 PM**, otherwise you cannot enter. Different dormitory managers have different requirements. Swiping campus card is required for entry and exit at any time.

**Q: Can I stay in the dormitory when there are no classes? Can I enter the dormitory at any time?**
A: Yes, you can stay in the dormitory when there are no classes. But pay attention to curfew time.

**Q: Are dormitory inspections strict?**
A: Freshman year is relatively strict; maintain cleanliness, bed curtains are not allowed. Daily duty for trash disposal and sweeping. Dormitory sockets except for charging cannot be used for other appliances (power limited).

**Q: Can I put pillows or similar items on the bed?**
A: Yes, but it's best to put them away during hygiene inspections.

**Q: Is the dormitory floor cement? Can I put floor stickers?**
A: Varies by dormitory building; you can but the effect is not great.

**Q: Can I keep pets in the dormitory?**
A: No, the university prohibits keeping pets in dormitories.

### Bedding

**Q: What are the bed dimensions?**
A: Standard dimensions are **90cm × 190cm**. If the bed length is insufficient, you can apply for an extension.

**Q: How is the university bedding? Is it recommended to use the university's?**
A: The university bedding quality is acceptable for normal use. Can be purchased as a set (ordering guide included with admission notice) or brought from home. If you don't want to carry heavy luggage at the start of school, you can directly use the university's.

**Q: Can I buy bedding items individually?**
A: Only **set purchase or full set from home** is supported; individual purchase is not available.

**Q: Must I use the university's unified bedding for military training?**
A: Not necessarily; you can use your own. Following university dimensions makes tidying up easier, but not following dimensions is also fine as long as you keep it tidy.

### Storage and Decoration

**Q: How big are the dormitory cabinets? Do they have dividers?**
A: Cabinet space is approximately 100cm×100cm×100cm, one per person (some dormitories have multiple per person), **no dividers**; storage supplies can be purchased separately. Wardrobes and storage cabinets are not separate.

**Q: Are there sockets beside each bed?**
A: Yes, but power is limited, only for low-power devices like phones and computers.

**Q: Can I put things on the dormitory walls? Can I put up wallpaper?**
A: You can stick adhesive hooks (be careful not to damage the walls) and hang posters. For wallpaper, consult your counselor after school starts.

**Q: Can I put floor mats in the dormitory?**
A: Not recommended; although possible, they are difficult to maintain.

**Q: Can I bring large storage boxes?**
A: Very large storage boxes are not recommended; you can buy them later based on available space. Items can only be placed in and on top of cabinets.

**Q: Are the dormitory ladders round or square?**
A: Square; you'll see when school starts.

### Appliances and Equipment

**Q: Do dormitories have air conditioning?**
A: **Since 2025, all dormitories have air conditioners installed**, achieving full air conditioning coverage! Say goodbye to summers without air conditioning.

**Q: Do dormitories have heating?**
A: **All are equipped with heating**; indoor temperatures are warm during Qingdao winters.

**Q: Are hair dryers allowed in dormitories?**
A: **Hair dryers are strictly prohibited in dormitories** (they are high-power heating appliances that will cause power cuts). After a power cut, you must go to the college and dormitory management office for processing to restore power. You can use your own hair dryer in **public areas downstairs** (sockets available). Bathhouses have scan-to-use hair dryers.

**Q: Can I bring a curling iron?**
A: **Curling irons and other heating appliances are strictly prohibited in dormitories.**

**Q: Do dormitories have drinking water dispensers?**
A: Generally equipped with public drinking water dispensers; **self-payment for water** is required, using the "Duocai Campus" APP.

**Q: Do dormitories have washing machines?**
A: Public washing machines are available in dormitory buildings; bring your own laundry detergent.

**Q: Are power strips provided in dormitories?**
A: The university **does not provide power strips**; **you need to purchase them yourself**, one socket per person.

**Q: Do dormitories have trash cans, brooms, and mops?**
A: Dormitories are equipped with basic cleaning tools; if not available, you can pick them up at designated locations; no need to bring your own.

**Q: Do dormitories have WiFi? How do I get campus network?**
A: After registering for campus network, you can connect to the university WiFi via account and password. Register through the "Jingcai Campus" official account. Campus network is free for the first month, then requires payment. Account is student ID, password is last ten digits of ID card.

### Other

**Q: Can I eat in the dormitory?**
A: You can bring food back to eat, but cannot cook yourself.

**Q: Does the dormitory support charging electric vehicles?**
A: Jinjialing Campus has many steps and slopes; bicycles are not recommended. There are no dedicated electric vehicle parking or charging areas. Bringing batteries into dormitories is not only against regulations, but after the class of 2023 graduates, there should be no legal electric vehicles left; only on-campus shared electric vehicles can be used.

**Q: Can I arrive early and stay in the dormitory?**
A: No, you must register according to the university's unified orientation schedule; dormitories are assigned on registration day.

**Q: Can I see photos after dormitory assignment? Are beds arranged by the university?**
A: Dormitory information is notified by the class monitor on orientation day. Beds are arranged by the university in advance. For exchanges, negotiate internally and then contact your counselor.

**Q: Do dormitories have study rooms?**
A: Some dormitory buildings are equipped with study rooms (see specific building notes above); additionally, you can use empty classrooms in teaching buildings and the library for studying.

**Q: Can I put things under the dormitory bed?**
A: Yes, but keep it clean and tidy.

**Q: Are there hooks in the dormitory to hang clothes?**
A: Adhesive hooks are allowed on dormitory walls for hanging clothes (be careful not to damage the walls).

**Q: Do I need to bring a thermos?**
A: Depends on personal situation; available at university stores, or you can bring your own.

**Q: What's the difference between bed curtains and mosquito nets?**
A: Jinjialing Campus **does not allow bed curtains**, but **mosquito nets are allowed**.

**Q: Can I install a bed desk?**
A: The university has not explicitly prohibited it; you can install one based on your situation.

**Q: Where can I air my quilt?**
A: You can air your quilt on the **balcony** or **downstairs railing**.
