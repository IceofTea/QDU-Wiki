## Sports Venues

### Fushan Campus

- **Three stadiums, two gymnasiums**: East, West, and North campuses each have a stadium (rubber track, including football field)
- **Indoor gymnasiums**: West Campus gymnasium (indoor basketball court), North Campus gymnasium (indoor basketball court); locked at night
- **Tennis courts**: Under the Student Activity Center
- **Roller skating rink**: In front of Qingda Theater
- **Basketball courts**:
  - 4-6 basketball courts next to Renyuan Dining Hall, West Campus
  - Basketball courts in front of Yingyuan dormitory buildings, East Campus
  - Basketball courts between teaching buildings and dormitory buildings, North Campus

### Jinjialing Campus

- **East Campus Sports Field**: Basketball courts (including over a dozen hoops), rubber track (including football field), basketball courts and football field are separate, **no lock time**
- **West Campus Sports Field**: Basketball courts (multiple hoops, located in front of female dormitory buildings), rubber track (including football field), **has lock time**
- **South of basketball courts**: **Skating rink** and **volleyball courts**

### Physical Education Classes and Sports Meet

Physical education classes are once a week, with physical fitness tests and final exams each semester. The sports meet is usually held in **April** with rich events:

- **Men**: 100m, 200m, 400m, 800m, 1500m, 5000m, 110m hurdles, relay, high jump, long jump, triple jump, shot put, discus, javelin
- **Women**: 100m, 200m, 400m, 800m, 1500m, 3000m, 100m hurdles, relay, high jump, long jump, triple jump, shot put, discus, javelin
- **Group events**: Tug-of-war, 1-minute rope skipping, face-to-face relay

## Bathhouse

Swipe campus card to use. Bathrooms have **partitions and shower curtains**, can place small baskets, with **single and double rooms**, large water flow, adjustable temperature. Middle area has lockers for clothes, outside has **hair dryers** for scan-to-pay use (strong airflow, adjustable temperature and wind speed).

!!! note "Official Opening Hours (Logistics Management Office)"
    According to the university's Logistics Management Office, unified opening hours for campus bathrooms are: **November 1 to March 31 of the following year: 10:30-21:30; April 1 to October 31: 10:30-22:30**. Officially published bathroom locations: Fushan Campus has Huiyuan Bathroom (south of Huiyuan Building 4), Yingyuan Bathroom (between dining hall and Yingyuan Building 11), Liuyuan Bathroom (behind Liuyuan Dining Hall), Hongyuan Bathroom (west side of Hongyuan Building 11 first floor is female bathroom, Hongyuan Building 4 first floor is male bathroom); Jinjialing Campus has West Campus Bathroom (east of Waterfront Restaurant), East Campus Bathroom (opposite East Campus Cainiao Station). Specific implementation may vary slightly by bathroom; the following is based on senior students' experience.

### Bathing Tips

- During military training, bathhouses are crowded; recommend bathing at off-peak times
- Can bring own hair dryer to public area downstairs (sockets available), but **cannot use in dormitory**
- Bathhouse hair dryer scan-to-pay: 10 minutes 1.50 yuan, 5 minutes 0.70 yuan
- Recommend bringing bath basket or small basket for toiletries
- Bathhouse does not provide toiletries; bring your own

## Campus Convenience Facilities

### Print Shops

**Jinjialing Campus**:
- Next to bathroom; across from hot water room, China Unicom business hall; Bishengke; under Building 3, West Campus

**Fushan Campus**:
- In front of Yingyuan Building 11; under Yingyuan Building 9; inside yellow wall compound, QDU Express Center, West Campus; near underground passage, West Campus; supermarket near campus hospital; supermarket in front of Huiyuan Building 2; Qingda Garden low-price supermarket; Tianyige, West Campus

### Express Delivery and Collection

Each campus has Cainiao Stations and express delivery collection points, covering mainstream couriers such as ZTO, YTO, Yunda, SF Express, STO, Tiantian, EMS, etc. Download the "Cainiao Guoguo" APP to scan and collect packages with pickup codes; generally collect within 2-3 days.

### Other Services

- **Key Duplication**: Inside Hongyuan low-price supermarket; at underground passage entrance, West Campus; north of Huiyuan Building 1
- **Shoe/Umbrella Repair**: Inside small building, West Campus market; entrance of Yingyuan dormitory
- **Phone/Computer Repair**: Inside fruit shop, West Campus market; near Student Activity Center; South Gate entrance, North Campus
- **Barbershops**: Distributed across multiple campuses
- **Optical Shops**: Building C, North Campus; under Student Activity Center, etc.
