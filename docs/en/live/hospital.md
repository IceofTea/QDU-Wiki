# Healthcare

## Campus Hospital

Qingdao University has a campus hospital on each campus; student enrollment and graduation physical examinations are conducted here. For any major university events, campus doctors are on standby.

### Campus Hospital Locations

| Campus | Location |
|------|------|
| **Fushan Campus (Staff Hospital)** | First Floor of **Shenxing Building**, West Campus |
| **Jinjialing Campus** | Between **Teaching Building 1 and 2**, West Campus |

### Treatment Process

Visiting the campus hospital with medical insurance card and medical record allows you to enjoy national discounts, reducing medical expenses.

**Standard Treatment Process**:
1. Present **medical insurance card, medical insurance record, ID card** at the registration room (**1.5 yuan**)
2. Go to the consultation room
3. Network settlement
4. Print invoice, pay
5. Collect medicine from pharmacy

**Specific Medicine Purchase Process** (no medical insurance coverage):
1. Accurately state the medicine name
2. Present medical insurance card, medical insurance record, ID card at the registration room (**0.5 yuan**)
3. Get prescription from doctor
4. Return to registration room for payment
5. Collect medicine from pharmacy

!!! note "Campus Hospital Costs"
    Campus hospital treatment is **not expensive**; daily common diseases are very convenient to treat at the campus hospital, with fewer people and lower prices. However, medicine prices may be slightly higher than wholesale prices.

## Insurance Recommendations

!!! warning "Strongly Recommended"
    **Be sure to join medical insurance after enrollment**, pay insurance premiums on time. Once insured, hospital treatment can be reimbursed partially. Additionally, **commercial insurance is strongly recommended** to avoid heavy financial pressure on families from huge treatment costs in case of accidental injuries.

The university will unify insurance notifications after school starts; participation is voluntary. It is recommended to purchase the university-recommended insurance if financial conditions allow, as it has wide coverage and low premiums.

## Freshman Physical Examination

- After school starts, **unified freshman physical examinations will be arranged**; no need to have a physical examination in advance
- Examination items include: seated forward bend, vital capacity, vision, height, weight, waist circumference, chest circumference, etc.
- **No eating before physical examination** (blood draw required)
- No need to wear underwear for examination

## Medical Tips

- Recommend bringing common medicines (cold medicine, anti-inflammatory drugs, anti-diarrhea medicine, fever reducers, band-aids, etc.); medicine prices near campus are slightly higher
- During military training, if feeling unwell, you can take sick leave with a medical record; don't push through
- Qingdao's climate is humid; lower-floor dormitories may consider preparing dehumidification supplies
- For mental health needs, contact the university's Psychological Health Education and Research Center
