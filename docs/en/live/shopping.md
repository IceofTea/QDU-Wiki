## Shopping Tips

- **Supermarket Prices**: On-campus supermarkets are at normal market prices; not cheaper or more expensive than outside.
- **Payment Methods**: Campus card, Alipay (WeChat Pay not supported).
- **Start-of-School Shopping**: Most dormitory supplies (basins, storage, etc.) will be sold by stalls in front of dormitory buildings on registration day; you can also bring only change of clothes and buy after arriving.
- **Express Delivery**: You can mail large items to the university express station before school starts to avoid inconvenience during travel.
- **Business Hours**: Hours vary by store; generally until 9-10 PM; some 7-Eleven and other convenience stores have longer hours.
