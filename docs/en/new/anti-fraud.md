# Anti-Fraud Guide

## Pickup Safety Tips

1. QDU shuttle buses will have **"QDU Welcome Pickup"** signs. Staff will wear work badges, student IDs, and academic affairs pickup IDs
2. **At bus and train stations, criminals may pretend to be welcome staff**. If taken away, you may be forced into hotel stays or pushed to buy overpriced items
3. If you encounter overly "enthusiastic" seniors, **ask for their credentials** and contact your own seniors to verify
4. School pickup locations: Qingdao Railway Station, Qingdao Railway Station North, Qingdao Long-distance Bus Terminal

## Travel Safety

1. **Buy tickets in advance (1-2 weeks)** to match your university registration schedule
2. On the train, **don't discuss your family situation with strangers**, especially home address and phone number
3. If you receive news about a family member "in trouble," **verify thoroughly**. This is a common scam
4. Keep your luggage close and carry valuables with you

## Property Safety

1. In an unfamiliar environment, **bring fewer valuables**. With modern logistics, laptops, cameras etc. can be sent later or mailed
2. Be wary of overly "helpful" seniors. **Carry your own bags with valuables** to prevent theft
3. **Don't buy school supplies from unofficial sources** (door-to-door sales of pens, notebooks are often overpriced, poor quality)
4. Campus management is chaotic at the start with many outsiders; watch your belongings

## Dormitory Safety

1. Dorms have **24/7 power**, 220V. **High-power appliances are strictly prohibited** (hair dryers, curling irons, electric cookers, etc.)
2. Using high-power appliances will trigger automatic power cut. Power can only be restored after reporting to college and dorm management
3. Since 2025, dorms have **full AC coverage** with heating in all rooms
4. Dorm outlets only work for charging; other appliances won't work due to power limits
5. Lock doors and windows when leaving, keep personal items safe
6. **Bring your own small lock** for dorm lockers

## Online Fraud Prevention

1. **Campus loans**: Never trust campus loan advertisements. Interest rates are extremely high with severe consequences. Contact your counselor for financial aid or student loans
2. **Click-farming jobs**: All "click-farm rebates" are scams, don't believe them
3. **Fake customer service**: If called by someone claiming to be Taobao/JD customer service about product issues and refunds, verify through official channels
4. **Impersonating teachers/classmates**: If someone on QQ/WeChat claims to be a teacher or classmate borrowing money, verify by phone
5. **Link scams**: Don't click links in unknown texts or messages, especially those requesting bank card information

## Sales Traps

1. **Reject all door-to-door sales** (English newspapers, cosmetics, cleaners, etc.) and don't let strangers into your dorm
2. Training agencies may promote computer certificate courses on campus. **The school doesn't organize unified training**, and teachers say it's useless
3. There may be campus SIM card promotions by carriers and student collaborators. **Choose based on needs; these are not official**
4. Don't buy school supplies from unofficial sources

## Personal Information Protection

1. **Don't lend** ID cards, bank cards, student IDs or other important documents
2. Don't fill in personal information on unknown websites or apps
3. Mark photocopies with "for XXX use only"
4. **Smart League Building, military recruitment info** etc. follow school announcements. Online rumors about "must register or can't sign up" are false

## Campus Card Security

1. Campus card is essential for dining, showering, and dorm access. **Don't lend it to others**
2. If your card is lost, **immediately** use the **"Youka" APP** to report it, or go to manual top-up points or self-service machines
3. After replacement, re-register access by bringing your student ID to Duxing Building Room 403

## Other Common Scams

1. **Shilaoren scenic area etc.**: People in cartoon costumes will try to drag you for photos — it's a scam. Never take photos with them
2. **"Helpful" people at stations**: They'll carry your luggage and guide you to transport, then demand high fees
3. **"Cheap branded goods" in dorms**: People selling "cheap" branded products (shampoo, cosmetics) are almost always selling fakes
4. **"Seniors" selling things**: After school starts, self-proclaimed seniors sell overpriced items with no quality guarantee

## Emergency Response

- If scammed or in danger, **contact your counselor immediately or call campus security**
- Security office: Fushan Campus **85951110**, Jinjialing Campus **85959110**
- Preserve evidence of scams (chat records, transfer records, etc.)
- Call **110** if necessary

## Anti-Fraud Summary

> One rule: **Don't trust, don't reveal, don't transfer, don't be greedy.**
>
> For anything involving money, ask your counselor or seniors first; reject all door-to-door sales; verify all online transfer requests.
