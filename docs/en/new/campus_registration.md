# Campus & Department Contacts

!!! tip "How to quickly find your campus"
    The most important thing before registration is to **figure out which campus you're going to**. You can search by your college in the campus list below based on your admission letter, or visit the [Colleges](../college/index.md) section for more information about each college.

---

## Which campus do I register at?

### Fushan Campus

> Fushan Campus (former Central Campus) is the **main campus**, where most colleges register. Address: **308 Ningxia Road, Shinan District, Qingdao**.

- **School of Marxism**
- **School of History**
- **School of Educational Science**
- **School of Physical Education**
- **School of Mathematics & Statistics**
- **College of Chemistry & Chemical Engineering**
- **School of Life Sciences**
- **College of Mechanical & Electrical Engineering**
- **School of Automation**
- **School of Electronic Information**
- **College of Textiles & Clothing**
- **Qingdao Medical School** (Clinical Medicine, Oral Medicine, Medical Laboratory, Pharmacy, Nursing, etc.)
- **School of Art**
- **Drake Joint College** (Sino-foreign cooperative program)

### Jinjialing Campus

> Jinjialing Campus (former East Campus) is the **main freshman campus**. Address: **62 Keda Zhili (West Area) / 93 Songling Road (East Area), Laoshan District, Qingdao**.

- **School of Economics**
- **Law School**
- **School of Politics & Public Administration**
- **School of Literature & Journalism**
- **School of Foreign Languages**
- **School of Physical Sciences**
- **School of Materials Science & Engineering**
- **School of Electrical Engineering**
- **College of Computer Science & Technology** (freshmen at Jinjialing, moving to Fushan from sophomore year)
- **School of Environmental & Geographic Sciences**
- **Business School**

!!! note "About Campuses"
    - **CS College** and other science/engineering departments: **freshmen at Jinjialing Campus (East Campus)**, moving to Fushan Campus from sophomore year
    - Other colleges follow the campus information on your admission letter. Above is reference for 2026 freshmen
