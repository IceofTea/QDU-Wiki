## Campus Network (WiFi)

### Basic Information

- The campus network is WiFi, network names: **QDU-1X** and **QDU-Web**
- Requires payment to activate, provider is China Mobile
- **Account**: Student ID
- **Password**: Last 10 digits of ID card
- **First month of campus network is free**
- A router can be used

### How to Apply

1. Find the link
2. Follow the instructions to complete registration and payment
3. Connect to QDU-1X or QDU-Web and log in with your account and password

!!! tip "About Campus Network"
    The campus network is not mandatory; choose according to your needs. At the start of school, you will receive a phone card from the university with complimentary campus data, which is usually sufficient if you only use mobile data.

## Mobile Phone Card

- The phone card provided by the university can be used once activated
- After arriving at school, you can bring your ID card or other valid documents to the **Mobile Business Hall** to apply for CMCC-EDU wireless internet, broadband, and in-network calls.
- You are not required to use the phone card included with the admission notice; you can **choose your own**. You can also apply on-site at the college on the registration day.
- All carriers (China Mobile, China Unicom, China Telecom) have similar speeds on campus; choose based on personal preference.

## Dormitory Network

- Some dormitories can have wired internet (consult customer service yourself)
- **Jinjialing Campus** West Campus can have China Telecom or China Mobile wired internet
- **Jinjialing Building No. 9** cannot have wired internet
- Yingyuan Dormitory: China Mobile has fast but unstable speed, China Unicom is stable but average speed
- Dormitories have **24-hour power supply**, voltage 220V, high-power appliances are strictly prohibited
- **Starting from 2025, all dormitories are fully equipped with air conditioning**, and all have heating.

## Library Internet Access

Students with a **library borrowing card** can access the internet for free in the electronic reading room on the second floor of the library.
