# Comprehensive Assessment

!!! tip "Content Note"
    The comprehensive assessment serves as an important basis for departments to evaluate scholarships and honors. **Department-specific details may vary**. This page is compiled using the **School of Computer Science and Technology, Class of 2022** comprehensive assessment standards (revised March 2023) as an example, for reference only.

## Score Composition

The comprehensive assessment totals 100 points, composed of five parts:

| Module | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Moral Quality (S1) | 15 points |
| Academic Quality (S2) | 65 points |
| Physical Quality (S3) | 8 points |
| Aesthetic Quality (S4) | 6 points |
| Labor Quality (S5) | 6 points |

An additional deduction item S0 is compiled by the department and class based on daily management records.

## Moral Quality (15 Points)

Moral score = Teacher evaluation (D1, 4 points) + Peer evaluation (D2, 4 points) + Moral scoring (D3, 7 points)

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Teacher Evaluation (D1) | Counselors, class teachers, etc. rate on a scale of Excellent/Good/Average/Poor, scoring 4/3/2/1 points respectively |
| Peer Evaluation (D2) | Youth University Study completion rate of 100% adds 2 points, 95% adds 1 point, 90% adds 0.5 points; class peer evaluation scores 2/1.5 points for top 30%/60% ranking, 1 point for the rest |
| Moral Scoring (D3) | Base score of 2 points (4 points for returning veterans), 2.5 points for serving the collective, other bonus items (self-study, party/league activities, blood donation, etc.) |

## Academic Quality (65 Points)

Academic score = Academic performance (Z1) + Innovation and entrepreneurship score (Z2, capped at 6 points cumulative)

- **Academic performance**: Primarily based on exam course grades
- **Innovation and entrepreneurship score**: Scored according to the "Qingdao University Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Practice Credit Scoring Standards." For details, see [Innovation Practice Credits](innovation_credit.md)

## Physical Quality (8 Points)

Physical score = Physical fitness test (T1, 4 points) + Campus health run (T2, 2 points) + Extracurricular physical activities (T3, 2 points)

- T1/T2 data are provided by the School of Physical Education; health run scores are proportional to distance (e.g., running 30km out of a 60km requirement earns half credit)
- Extracurricular physical activities: Department-level participation adds 0.2 points, university-level adds 0.2 points, award winners earn bonus points by level, capped at 2 points cumulative

## Aesthetic Quality (6 Points)

Aesthetic score = Art practice (M1, 2 points) + Cultural practice (M2, 2 points) + Classic reading (M3, 2 points)

- Department-level art/cultural practice: 0.5 points per participation, 1 point per award; university-level and above: 1 point per participation, 2 points per award, capped at 2 points cumulative
- Classic reading: Completing 2 classic books per semester, submitting a reading reflection earns 1 point per piece, book club sharing earns 0.5 points per session, capped at 2 points cumulative

## Labor Quality (6 Points)

Labor score = Daily labor (L1, 3 points) + Social practice (L2, 3 points)

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Daily Labor (L1) | Dormitory hygiene averages: 95+ points earns 2.5 points, 90–94 earns 2 points, 85–89 earns 1 point, 80–84 earns 0.5 points, below 80 earns 0 points; university-level or above commendations add 0.5 points per instance, capped at 3 points cumulative |
| Social Practice (L2) | University/department-organized social practice: 0.5 points per participation; university-level commendation 2 points per instance, department-level 1 point per instance; volunteer activities: 0.2 points per hour, semester cap of 1 point |

## Deduction Item (S0)

Violations of dormitory management regulations resulting in criticism or adverse impact: 0.2 points per instance, 0.4 for the second instance, 0.8 for the third, doubling each time.

## General Rules

- Within the same semester, the same achievement (work, grade, project, deed, etc.) receiving awards at different levels is **counted only at the highest score, with no cumulative scoring**
- All bonus items require submitting certificate copies; award certificates must bear the "Qingdao University" or department seal to be valid
- Team projects: For those with ranked positions, the first-ranked person receives full points, others receive 1/2; for unranked team projects, all members receive full points
- **Fraud during the comprehensive assessment process results in a moral score of zero**
- Comprehensive scores are保留 to two decimal places
