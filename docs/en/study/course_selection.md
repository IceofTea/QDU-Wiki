# Course Selection Guide

!!! tip "Policy Basis"
    This page is compiled from the "Qingdao University Undergraduate-Graduate Integrated Teaching Management Platform Course Selection Operation Guide." The original document contains interface screenshots; only text steps are retained here.

## Login

- Login address: <http://xjw.qdu.edu.cn/>
- It is recommended to use a **desktop computer**; mobile devices may experience missing page elements
- **Username** is your student ID; **password** is your Smart Campus password
- Forgotten password: Log in to [Smart Campus](http://ehall.qdu.edu.cn/new/index.html) to reset via bound mobile phone SMS, or contact Smart Campus at **0532-85955678**

## Entering Course Selection

1. After logging in, click the "**Course Selection Center**" icon, or navigate from the left menu: **Training Management → Course Management → Student Course Selection Center**
2. Confirm the course selection round name and start time, then click "**Enter Course Selection**" in the operation column

## Course Selection Center Interface

| Area | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Area 1 | Schedule of selected courses (does not refresh immediately after selection; click "Default Period Mode" in the upper left corner to manually refresh) |
| Area 2 | Selected credit statistics (click "Refresh" to manually update) |
| Area 3 | Available course tabs |

Area 3 contains three tabs:

- **Course Selection Credit Status**: Maximum course selection credit limit for the current semester and current selections
- **Professional Elective Course Selection**: Elective courses within your major's current semester plan
- **General Elective Course Selection**: All university-wide general elective courses offered this semester

## Course Selection and Withdrawal

- The system has **pre-arranged required courses** within your plan. First click "View Selection Results and Withdraw" to verify the pre-arranged results
- **Selecting courses**: Based on the class schedule in the system, search for courses in the "Professional Elective Course Selection" or "General Elective Course Selection" tab, then click **[Select]** on the corresponding row
- **Withdrawing from courses**: In "Selection Results," select the course and click **[Withdraw]** on the corresponding row
- After completing course selection, click the "**Safe Exit Course Selection**" button in the upper right corner to exit the course selection interface

## Viewing Class Schedule

- On the system homepage, click [My Schedule] → [Semester Theory Schedule]
- The schedule is divided into two parts: courses with time and location shown in each period slot; courses without time and location shown in the notes
