# Exams and Grades

## Freshman English Placement Exam

As per tradition, there is an **English placement exam** after school starts, covering listening and reading. It is somewhat difficult, harder than the gaokao. Question types are **similar to CET-4**, serving as a **placement test and allocation of spots for the College English Competition**. If you have the capacity, it's worth preparing in advance. Graduate students also take this exam; a high score can exempt you from graduate English courses.

## CET-4 and CET-6 Exams

- You can register for CET-4 in the **spring semester of freshman year**
- CET-6 can be taken after passing CET-4; if you miss one exam, you cannot register for the next one and must wait until the one after that. Fushan test centers for CET-4 are hard to secure, and CET-6 spots are even harder
- The listening section requires a **radio**; the university distributes them uniformly (pre-order through the orientation system), or you can purchase your own

## Final Exams

- The last two weeks of each semester are **exam weeks**
- Multiple classes take exams in the same classroom, seated every other seat, generally by student ID number, though some proctors don't enforce this
- Final exam grades are linked to various evaluations and honors

### Exam Discipline

!!! danger "Cheating is Strictly Prohibited"
    Failing an exam allows for a makeup exam, but **if cheating is caught by an inspector**, you will face disciplinary action with serious consequences! Students who applied for a deferred exam may take the makeup exam.

According to the "Shandong Province Regular Higher Education Credit System Management Regulations":
- **Absent from exam**: Students may not participate in the makeup or deferred exam for the relevant course and can only retake it
- A student who fails to attend the normal course examination without valid reason is considered absent from the exam

## Exam Rankings

- Exam year rankings are **only within your own major**
- Some grade requirements consider class proportions

## Failing a Course and Makeup Exams

| Situation | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| First time failing | A **makeup exam** is arranged at the beginning of the new semester; passing the makeup exam records only 60 points in your file |
| Failing the makeup exam | Must **retake the course**, paying the course fee again and studying with the next cohort of students |
| Makeup exam opportunity | You only get **one** makeup exam chance per failure |

### Consequences of Failing

!!! warning "Failing Has Serious Consequences"
    - Affects **postgraduate recommendation** eligibility
    - Affects **scholarship** evaluation
    - Affects **party membership** and other evaluation activities
    - Affects **student council** elections
    - Recorded in your personal file, impacting future employment

## Scholarships

Scholarships are evaluated at the beginning of each semester for the previous semester, ranked by **comprehensive assessment scores**, with higher scores having priority.

| Scholarship Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **First-Class Scholarship** | Top 5% in comprehensive assessment ranking |
| **Second-Class Scholarship** | Top 15% in comprehensive assessment ranking |
| **Third-Class Scholarship** | Top 30% in comprehensive assessment ranking |
| **Progress Scholarship** | Top ranking improvement within the department |

Scholarship quotas are allocated by the department to each grade, then distributed by department enrollment proportions.

## Postgraduate Recommendation (Exemption from Entrance Exam)

Postgraduate recommendation refers to **recommending outstanding graduating undergraduates for exemption from the national graduate entrance exam** (abbreviated as "tuimian"), directly entering the re-examination phase. Qingdao University's recommendation process follows the **"Qingdao University Implementation Measures for Recommending Outstanding Graduating Undergraduates for Exemption from Graduate Entrance Examination" (Document No. QDU-Jiao [2021] 21)**, with the Academic Affairs Office publishing annual notices each September. The following content is compiled based on the 2025 recommendation work documents.

> **How many postgraduate recommendation spots are there?** Quotas are not set by a fixed proportion but are allocated by the university based on each school's graduating class size, major construction status, teaching achievement awards, and student advanced study rates, **distributed to departments each year**, then further distributed to majors based on graduating class enrollment and training quality. To learn your major's specific quota, check the "Recommendation Implementation Plan" published by your department in early September.

### Recommendation Categories

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Regular Recommendation** | Open to most students, recommended based on comprehensive score ranking |
| **Counselor Recommendation Plan** | For students with the qualities and willingness to serve as student counselors, following the full-time counselor selection process |

> Each student may only obtain **one category** of recommendation eligibility and cannot apply for multiple.

### Basic Requirements (Hard Threshold)

1. **Current university graduating undergraduate** enrolled in the national regular full-time undergraduate admissions plan (excluding college-to-university transfers and second bachelor's degrees)
2. **Moral quality assessment score of "good" or above**, with no record of violations or disciplinary actions
3. Completed and passed all courses specified in the professional training plan for the corresponding year, with **no failing grades in required courses**

!!! warning "Failing a Required Course = Automatic Disqualification"
    If even one required course has a failing record, you cannot apply for postgraduate recommendation. Failing not only affects evaluations but is also a veto criterion for postgraduate recommendation.

### Academic and Language Requirements for Regular Recommendations

**Grade Ranking (Exam Course Average GPA)**:

| Student Type | Ranking Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Regular Students | 4-year programs: top three years; 5-year programs: top four years. Exam course average GPA ranking no lower than top **30%** of the major |
| Innovation Experiment Classes, Excellence Talent Plan Majors | May be relaxed to top **40%** |
| Students with genuine special academic expertise | May be relaxed to top **50%** |

**Language Requirements**:

| Student Type | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Non-foreign language majors (English) | CET-6 score ≥ **426**, or IELTS ≥ 6 / TOEFL ≥ 80 |
| Non-foreign language majors (other languages) | Corresponding language CET-4 ≥ 80, or CET-6 ≥ 60 |
| Foreign language majors | Passing the corresponding language professional CET-4 exam |
| Arts and sports majors | CET-4 score ≥ 426 |

### How Comprehensive Score Is Calculated (Core)

Rankings are based on **comprehensive score**, composed of academic performance and comprehensive development quality assessment score:

**Comprehensive Score = Academic Performance × 90% + Comprehensive Development Quality Assessment Score × 10%**

- **Academic Performance**: All **exam course** grades during enrollment, out of 100 points. Accounting for 90%, this is decisive.
- **Comprehensive Development Quality Assessment Score**: Out of 100 points, composed of social practice and special academic expertise, accounting for 10%.

!!! tip "Grades Are King"
    Comprehensive development accounts for at most 10%, and the conditions for earning high scores are stringent (see below). For most students, **ranking among the top in exam course grades** is the most reliable path to postgraduate recommendation. Competitions and volunteer work are merely bonuses.

#### Comprehensive Development Quality Assessment Components (Out of 100)

| Section | Weight | Sub-items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Social Practice** | 30% | Military service (10%), volunteer service (10%), international organization internships (10%) |
| **Special Academic Expertise** | 70% | "Internet+" competition direct bonus, Challenge Cup (20%), other authoritative competitions (25%), research achievements (25%) |

Each sub-item is out of 100 points, converted by weight and summed; each sub-item may accumulate **at most three representative works**, with the same achievement/award counted only at the highest level without duplication. Scores exceeding 100 are capped at 100.

- **Social Practice**: Military service of 2+ years scores 80 points, "Outstanding Soldier" title scores 100 points; national/provincial "Most Beautiful Volunteer," "Outstanding Youth Volunteer," "Three Goes to the Countryside" outstanding individuals, etc. scored by level; international organization internships of 3+ months earn points.
- **"Internet+" Competition**: A direct bonus item, converted at 70% and added to the special academic expertise category. National gold awards for top 3 positions: 100/80/60 points; silver: 80/60/40; bronze: 60/40/20.
- **Challenge Cup**: Converted at 20%. National gold awards for top 3: 100/80/60 points; silver: 80/60/40; bronze: 60/40/20.
- **Other Authoritative Competitions**: Converted at 25%. Based on the Chinese Higher Education Society's "National Ordinary University Student Competition Ranking" for the recommendation year; top awards for competitions on the ranking list capped at 50 points, unlisted competitions capped at 40 points. Scoring standards are set by each department.
- **Research Achievements**: Converted at 25%. Papers must be published with Qingdao University as the first author institution and the student as independent or first author. Scoring standards are set by each department.

> **How are key team members determined for team competitions?** For individual competitions, participants are key members; for team competitions where positions can be identified, the top 3 positions are taken with decreasing scores; for team competitions where positions cannot be identified, all team members share equally. The total points for all key team members must not exceed 2.4 times that of individual competition participants at the same level.

### Special Academic Expertise Defense

Students with special academic expertise must submit the "Special Academic Expertise Review and Appraisal Form" and supporting materials, participating in a **public defense** organized by the department (typically no fewer than 5 experts, associate professor rank or above). The entire defense is audio and video recorded, with results publicly announced. **Those who do not pass the review or defense will not have their special academic expertise score counted.**

### Recommendation Work Process (Using 2025 as an Example)

| Time | Step |
| --- | --- |
| Early September | University publishes recommendation notice; department announces implementation plan (including major quotas) |
| Before September 11 | Department submits recommendation work plan and working group list |
| September 9–18 | Students submit application forms and materials; those with special expertise participate in defense; department reviews, determines candidates, and **publicly announces for 7 days** |
| September 19–24 | University reviews materials, leadership group deliberates and announces |
| September 25–26 | Recommendation list submitted to the Ministry of Education's recommendation system |

### Important Notes

- When higher-ranked students waive their recommendation quota, they must sign a written waiver, and quotas are **filled sequentially** by comprehensive score ranking
- Those who have obtained recommendation eligibility but receive disciplinary action or cannot graduate normally before graduation will have their **qualification revoked** by the university and reported to the Ministry of Education
- Fabrication of application materials results in immediate disqualification upon discovery and strict disciplinary action
- Those with immediate family members participating in the current year's selection must **proactively回避**; non-immediate family members must proactively report
- The recommendation process involves department and university-level public announcements, subject to supervision by the university's Discipline Inspection Commission (Supervision Office) throughout the process, with real-name reporting accepted

## Physical Education Classes and Fitness Tests

- Physical education classes are **once per week**
- Each semester has **fitness tests** and **final exams**
- Physical education classes carry credits; failing requires a makeup exam

The university sports meet is generally held in **April**, with events including:
