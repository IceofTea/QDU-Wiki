## What Is This

Innovation practice credits refer to the credits earned by undergraduates during their enrollment period through achieving results in **academic competitions, professional qualification exams, innovation and entrepreneurship training projects, publishing papers or works, scientific research, inventions, academic exchanges, professional certification, and entrepreneurial practice**, which are recognized by the university. They serve as the grade basis for the university's required "**Innovation and Entrepreneurship Practice**" course (3 credits).

- Applied for and recognized through the university's **"Second Classroom" online platform**, with results recorded on the "Second Classroom Transcript"
- **Students who do not earn at least 3 points will not meet graduation requirements** ("Innovation and Entrepreneurship Practice" is a required course, 3 credits)
- These are the 3 credits under the "Innovation and Entrepreneurship" category of the Second Classroom, independent of and non-duplicative with other Second Classroom modules

### Significance and Purpose

- **National Initiative**: Innovation and entrepreneurship education is a key direction for national higher education reform. The State Council and Ministry of Education have issued multiple directives supporting student innovation and entrepreneurship training projects; innovation practice credits are the practical implementation
- **Driving Growth**: Encouraging students to apply classroom knowledge to real-world scenarios such as competitions, research, and entrepreneurship, enhancing innovative spirit, entrepreneurial awareness, and practical capabilities
- **Graduation Necessity**: It is a clearly defined required course; failing to earn 3 credits affects graduation — it's a "must-achieve" hard credit
- **Postgraduate Recommendation Bonus**: Achievements such as competition awards, published papers, patents, and software copyrights not only count toward Second Classroom scoring but also serve as important credentials for postgraduate recommendation and scholarship evaluation

## Grade Conversion

In the graduation semester, based on the total points recognized on the "Second Classroom" platform, the course grade for "Innovation and Entrepreneurship Practice" (required course, 3 credits) is recorded:

| Recognized Total Points | Course Grade |
| --- | --- |
| 10 or above | 100 |
| 8 or above but less than 10 | 95 |
| 5 or above but less than 8 | 90 |
| 3 or above but less than 5 | 85 |
| Less than 3 | 55 |

## Recognition Process

1. The student fills out the Innovation Practice Credit application form and submits **valid proof materials** (certificates, papers, project completion certificates, etc.) for innovation and entrepreneurship achievements to their department
2. The department reviews the application materials and recognizes the corresponding points based on the content and level of the achievements
3. The department uploads the recognized data to the "Second Classroom" online platform, recording it on your "Second Classroom Transcript"
4. In the graduation semester, based on the total recognized points, the department enters the "Innovation and Entrepreneurship Practice" course grade

> Each department may add its own innovation and entrepreneurship practice achievement items based on its disciplinary characteristics, subject to approval by the Academic Affairs Office. Contact: School of Innovation and Entrepreneurship (phone 85957378).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can language and computer certificates be stacked?**
A: No. Certificates of the same type are only counted at the **highest point level**, with no cumulative scoring.

**Q: Can a driver's license earn points?**
A: No. A driver's license is not within the scope of "National Vocational Skills Standards Directory" professional qualifications, nor does it belong to the above scoring standards. Although online jokes say a driver's license can earn points, Qingdao University's current documents do **not** include this item.

**Q: If I won multiple awards in the same competition, how is it calculated?**
A: The same competition in the same year is only counted once at the **highest award level**.

**Q: How are team award points distributed?**
A: The team leader or first-ranked member receives double points; other members receive single points.

**Q: What counts as a "national-level registration qualification certificate"?**
A: This refers to professional qualifications listed in the "National Vocational Skills Standards Directory," certificates issued by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, verifiable on the Vocational Skills Certification Query System (`http://zscx.osta.org.cn`); "professional qualification certificates" are issued by industry administrative departments or industry associations.

**Q: What counts as a core journal?**
A: Each department determines journal classification standards based on its own disciplinary characteristics. Please consult your department's academic affairs office for specifics.

!!! danger "Integrity Reminder"
    Those who **fabricate information** during the application and recognition process will have their recognized points revoked and be subject to strict disciplinary action per regulations. You can earn points gradually, but once your credibility is compromised, it becomes a serious problem.
