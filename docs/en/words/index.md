## Messages

### 炎黄YH

Everyone's university life has its own meaning. Focus more on value, not on cost. Students who study hard for postgraduate recommendation **sacrifice** entertainment to **gain** achievement, while those who slack off with "60 points is enough" **sacrifice** learning to **gain** happiness. As long as you don't engage in endless internal friction, and can feel that you were once happy at graduation, that's the best result.

> —— 炎黄YH

### 玉环YH (Class of 2024 Software Engineering)

Make choices carefully, and also be prepared to bear the bitter consequences. And don't regret.
Use AI deeply, but absolutely absolutely do not outsource thinking.
Students who choose to employ should intern early, work on truly valuable projects, and pay attention to the accumulation of basic knowledge and skills, especially mathematics, algorithms, and 408 professional courses.
When looking for jobs, internal referrals and official website applications are much more efficient than BOSS Zhipin. Making more friends and participating in technical communities is beneficial without harm, especially in some relatively vertical fields (such as competitions, certain subdivision industry technologies, etc.).
Freshman year might be the most leisurely and happy time in university...

> —— 玉环YH
